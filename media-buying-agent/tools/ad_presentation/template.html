1 · The offer

One clear first step, framed for golfers

The $49 Pain-Free Golf Visit

$150$49
Only 10 a month

It's your $49 Discovery Visit, named for golfers. 30 minutes, in person, one-on-one with Brian: their history, a movement screen, and a straight answer on what's going on, whether PT can help, how long it takes, what it costs and what to do at home. Paid when they book.

"An opportunity to see if we are a good fit for each other." Your words, used across the campaign.

Why this offer
  • Golf is the hook. The deepest fear we found isn't pain, it's having to give up the game.
  • "Pain-Free Golf" covers whatever is limiting their game (back, hips, shoulder, elbow, knee), not just one body part.
  • The $150 anchor is your real full-evaluation price, so $49 reads as the deal it is.
  • The only urgency is real: 10 visits a month. No fake countdowns, ever.
  • Paying $49 at booking filters for golfers who are serious, so your schedule fills with people who show up.
  • The TPI screen stays separate. The ads use your TPI footage to show your golf expertise, never as part of the $49 visit.
2 · The funnel

Where each golfer is, and which ad meets them there

Golfers don't go from "my back is tight" to "book a visit" in one step. They move through stages of awareness, and an ad works best when it does the one job of the stage the viewer is in. That's why there are ten different ads instead of one ad shown ten times. Meta shows each ad to the people most likely to respond to it.
