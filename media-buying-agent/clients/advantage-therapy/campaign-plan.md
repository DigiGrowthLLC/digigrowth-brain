## 5. Budget-reality math

- **Budget:** $25/day (house minimum) × 28 days = **$700**. The goal window is 6 weeks; at $25/day, 6 weeks would be $1,050. Leads from week 4 still book during weeks 4-6, and database-reactivation bookings count toward the goal.
- **Arithmetic (booked reviews = spend ÷ CPL × lead→booking rate):**

| Scenario | Cost per lead | Leads ($700) | Lead → booked | Booked reviews |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative (DigiGrowth KPI ceiling) | $25 | 28 | 20% | **~6** |
| Middle | $12 | 58 | 15% | **~9** |
| Aug-like (Instant Form, $3-5 CPL) | $5 | 140 | 8% | **~11** |

- **Read:** 7-10 is **realistic but tight** on $700. It depends far more on lead→booking rate (AI agent speed, the qualifier question) than on CPL. Cheap Instant Form leads book at lower rates, which is why the form adds the "what have you tried" question and the higher-intent review step.
- **Trade-off for Dylan:** (a) run $25/day for 4 weeks and stop (≈$700), or (b) run $25/day for the full 6 weeks (≈$1,050), or (c) $25/day for 4 weeks with a **week-2 checkpoint**: under 3 booked reviews by day 14 → fix creative or follow-up; on pace → extend to 6 weeks. **Recommended: (c).**
- **Learning phase:** at ~$25/day the ad set won't reach 50 leads/week at higher CPLs; expect "learning limited". Judge by cost per booked review, not by Meta's learning status.

## 8. Measurement plan

**North-star metric:** booked free reviews (Google Meet), counted from GHL bookings in the dashboard. Target **7-10 in 6 weeks**.

| Metric | Target | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per lead (Instant Form) | ≤ $12 (ceiling $25) | Meta |
| Lead → booked review | ≥ 15% | Dashboard (AI agent bookings) |
| Cost per booked review | ≤ $80 (ceiling $100) | Spend ÷ bookings |
| Show rate on the Meet | ≥ 70% | Phil, weekly |
| Review → first paid visit | Baseline (unknown today); track from week 1 | Phil, weekly |
| Time to first text | < 5 minutes | AI agent logs |

**Weekly checkpoint (every Monday)**
1. **Spend spread:** healthy = 6+ of the 10 ads getting spend. Stuck in 2-3 ads → the next batch adds new *formats* from his IG (carousel, founder, case reel), not variants of the winners.
2. **Cost per booked review** vs. $80/$100: judge the ad set on this, not single-ad CPL.
3. **Lead → booked rate:** under 10% means follow-up or lead quality (read the "what have you tried" answers and AI agent threads), not the ads.
4. **Show rate and review → first visit** from Phil: low show → fix reminder and confirmation wording.
5. **Which "what have you tried" answer books best:** that segment gets the next batch.

**Video read (hook + hold together):** hook rate = 3-second views ÷ impressions (target ≥ 25%); hold rate = ThruPlays ÷ 3-second views (target ≥ 10%). Low hook → change the title card / first frame. Good hook, low hold → tighten the middle of the cut. Good hook and hold, few leads → strengthen the offer card and CTA.

**Kill rules:** don't kill an ad only for a worse CPL while it's getting spend. Do kill policy rejections (fix and relaunch) and any ad with 0 leads after ~$40 spend. Don't pause the ad set to "reset learning" in the first 2 weeks.

**Week-2 checkpoint (day 14, decided at G6):** under 3 booked reviews → fix (new creative from his IG alternates if CPL > $25; tighter follow-up if lead → booked < 10%). On pace → extend $25/day to the full 6 weeks (~$1,050).

**Scaling path:** when the first batch plateaus (frequency > 2.5 and CPL rising with steady CTR), open the next segment with new people and formats from his IG (e.g. a neck-headache batch, an athlete batch), not more variants of the same ads.
