## Rules (Dylan, 2026-11-09, campaign build gates G1-R3)

- **Segments:** (A) active adults who keep getting pulled out of their sport (run, ski, climb, ride, lift) and (B) chronic pain, tried everything. Not prehab/post-surgery and not elite athletes this campaign, although Aaron serves both.
- **Budget and goal:** $30/day for 30 days, aiming for 7-10 paid $49 assessments in 30 days.
- **Lead path:** Meta Instant Form, then the AI text agent ("Maya") texts the $49 booking link (paid-offer flow, the patient pays at booking). Not the landing page as the ad destination.
- **Offer on the funnel:** $49 one-on-one 60-min assessment, "normally $120", paid at booking, limited spots each month. Ongoing session prices are never quoted in ads or texts.
- **Name MAT in hooks** (Dylan, R3): "MAT" / "Muscle Activation Techniques" can be the curiosity hook. Explain it only in plain words ("finds the muscles that stopped doing their job"), never as a technical walkthrough.
- **Testimonials:** Aaron's own patients only, first name only (Max, Amie, Annette, Sando, Lisa, Gabby, Helen, Nicolene, Marc). Research quotes from Reddit/YouTube/forums are language input, never shown as testimonials. Helen's review is pre-surgery (TKR): fine as skeptic proof, but the campaign doesn't target prehab.
- **Don't compete on reviews:** every top Boulder PT clinic is rated 4.9-5.0 with 140-717 reviews. Lead with the different promise (one therapist, the whole hour; for people regular PT didn't fix), never with a star count.
- **Never promise "no more visits":** Aaron's model is weekly for about 6 weeks, then monthly maintenance ($800-5,000 per patient).
- **Imaging (Aaron, 2026-11-10):** many of his patients have degeneration / "wear and tear" on their MRI and still do well. Never imply a *normal* scan is needed or that degeneration rules anyone out. Imaging angles speak to both ("normal" or "wear and tear") and say a scan can't show which muscles are switching on.
- **Words to avoid:** "root cause", "get back to doing what you love", "unlock your body's potential", "holistic".

## Offer (approved 2026-11-09, Dylan + Aaron)

**The Why-It-Keeps-Coming-Back Assessment.** $49, normally $120 (his real 60-min session price), paid at booking via the link the AI text agent sends. Overrides the free-consult default for this client: ads, the AI agent and the funnel all sell this offer, with the price included.
- **Included:** 60 minutes one-on-one with Dr. Aaron Shaver, DPT, the whole time · his full story and everything he's tried · a muscle-by-muscle Muscle Activation Techniques (MAT) test to find the muscles that stopped doing their job · hands-on work on what it finds, then a re-test, so he sees and feels what changed in the same visit · a clear plan.
- **No guarantee, no free option, no bonus** (Dylan, O1). Never promise a health outcome; "see what changed" describes the re-test, not a cure.
- **Urgency (real capacity):** "Dr. Aaron sees every patient himself, so there are only 10 new-patient spots a month." Goes in every ad CTA, one headline per ad, the end card, the Instant Form, the AI agent brief and the booking-invite texts; not in reminders. No countdowns or "only N left".
- **Standard CTA:** "Only 10 new-patient spots a month. Book your $49 Why-It-Keeps-Coming-Back Assessment."
- **Ski season** is an ad angle for Segment A ("before the lifts open"), not an offer deadline.
