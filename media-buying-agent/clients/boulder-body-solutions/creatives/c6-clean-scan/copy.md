## C6 · Whatever your scan says
**Revision 2026-11-10 (Aaron's feedback):** many of his patients have degeneration ("wear and tear") on imaging and still do well, so the ad must not imply you need a *normal* scan. The angle is now "whatever your scan says, it can't show which muscles are switching on". It speaks to both groups (told "normal", told "wear and tear") and never suggests degeneration rules anyone out. No outcome promises: "that part can change" describes the possibility, not a result.
