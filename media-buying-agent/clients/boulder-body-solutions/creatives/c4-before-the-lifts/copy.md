# c4-before-the-lifts (Boulder Body Solutions, batch 1, approved 2026-11-09)

Every ad sells: **The Why-It-Keeps-Coming-Back Assessment, $49 (normally $120), 10 new-patient spots this month.** Capture: Meta Instant Form → AI text agent sends the $49 booking link.
**CTA button: "Sign up"** for every ad. Don't use "Book Now" with an Instant Form: Meta rejected that pairing on Advantage's drafts ("Invalid value field lead_gen_form_id for CTA type: BOOK_NOW").
**Rules applied to every line:** no emoji, no em dashes, situation hooks (never "do you have X pain"), only Aaron's real patients as proof (first names), AI actors speak as people in the situation (never "Aaron fixed me"), no outcome promises, spot line in every CTA, and no "root cause" or "get back to what you love".
