Every ad sells: **The Why-It-Keeps-Coming-Back Assessment, $49 (normally $120), 10 new-patient spots this month.** Capture: Meta Instant Form → AI text agent sends the $49 booking link.
**CTA button: "Sign up"** for every ad. Don't use "Book Now" with an Instant Form: Meta rejected that pairing on Advantage's drafts ("Invalid value field lead_gen_form_id for CTA type: BOOK_NOW").
**Rules applied to every line:** no emoji, no em dashes, situation hooks (never "do you have X pain"), only Aaron's real patients as proof (first names), AI actors speak as people in the situation (never "Aaron fixed me"), no outcome promises, spot line in every CTA, and no "root cause" or "get back to what you love".

## C8 · Full 6-beat story (45-60s)
**Brief:** concept = the long tried-everything story | creative type = AI UGC long-form story | angle = managed but never fixed → the test was different | stage = solution-aware | lead = Connection | avatar = active adult ~45, climber/hiker, shoulder | visual = woman at a climbing gym or trailhead, then at home; cutaways to review cards and the end card
**Built on:** P38 RunLab (52 months: one long story → "the difference was the test" → back to planning trips). Quote: "I learned to manage the pain but it didn't go away" (runner forum); reviews from Max, Sando and Lisa.

**Script (9:16 + 4:5, ~55s, 6 beats)**
1. Hook 0-4s (caption "MANAGED IT. NEVER FIXED IT."): "I spent three years managing my shoulder. Never fixing it."
2. Story 4-18s: "PT twice. Massage every month. A thousand band exercises. It'd get better, I'd climb a bit harder, and it'd flare right back up. I started skipping trips."
3. Why this works 18-32s: "Then a friend told me about MAT. The idea is simple. When one muscle stops firing, the one next to it does double duty and gets cranky. Stretching and massage calm the cranky one. Nobody was checking the quiet one."
4. What the visit is 32-40s: "There's a DPT in Boulder, Dr. Aaron Shaver, who tests exactly that. A full hour, one on one. Your story, the muscle test, hands-on work, a re-test and a plan."
5. Reviews 40-50s (cards): Sando: "through an hour long session with him he had it moving better than it had been in months." / Lisa: "I was able to see how my body reacted differently." / Max: "countless physical therapists that were unable to treat me effectively."
6. CTA 50-55s: "It's called the Why-It-Keeps-Coming-Back Assessment. Forty-nine dollars. He only takes 10 new people a month."
