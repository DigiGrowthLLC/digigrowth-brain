## 1. Segment snapshot
A committed golfer (often a low or mid handicapper, plays a lot, "got the golf bug bad"), now feeling his age in his back. The pain shows up after range sessions and swing changes, the morning after a round, or mid-round, and he treats it himself: swing tweaks from YouTube, ibuprofen before the round, stretching his lower back, the occasional chiropractor. Golf is his social life and identity ("hanging out with the boys"). **Trigger moment:** a round he couldn't finish or had to pull out of, or the night before a round wondering if he can play (Y-tomorrow, F-scramble), or the first time he seriously thinks about taking a break or quitting (Y-break, Y-quit).

## 2. Pains, ranked
1. **Fear of losing golf itself** (most intense): Y-break, Y-nomore, Y-retire, Y-quit, Y-boys, F-fullround.
2. **Pain during and after playing, getting worse with more golf**: Y-mofo, Y-older, Y-advil, F-scramble.
3. **Their game gets worse because of it** (shorter swing, poor distance, swing changes to protect the back): F-fullround ("pathetic swing and poor distance"), Y-nomore ("take the driver out of the bag"), Y-videos.
4. **Confusion and conflicting advice** (pros, YouTube, instructors): Y-break ("not listening to another so called professional again"), Y-videos, Y-still.

## 3. What they've already tried, and why it fell short
- **Pills before/after rounds**: Y-advil, F-pills. Masks it, doesn't fix it.
- **Swing tips from YouTube or a pro**: Y-tomorrow, Y-videos, Y-break. Sometimes helps, often causes new pain.
- **Stretching/strengthening the lower back itself**: Y-victim, Y-still. Doesn't work when the back is compensating for stiff hips/upper back.
- **Chiropractor, steroids, generic PT**: F-fullround, F-steroids, P-Barry, P-Andrew, P-check. "Gave me exercises", "to no avail".

## 4. Dream outcome in their words
- Play a full round (18) without pain and without pills: F-fullround, Y-advil.
- Hit balls with "no sore back" and get distance back ("an extra club of distance"): Y-distance, F-fullround.
- Keep playing for years ("extend my golfing career", not "retire"): Y-break, Y-retire.
- Loose, not tight: P-nervous ("loose muscles and tight muscles is a night and day difference"), Y-victim.

## 5. Objections and fears
