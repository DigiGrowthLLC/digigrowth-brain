## 5. Budget math
$900 for 30 days. PT lead-form leads typically cost $15-30 on Meta; golf-targeted local audiences sit at the higher end. At ~$22/lead that's ~40 leads. If the text agent gets 20-25% of them to book and pay $49 (paid booking filters harder than a free consult), that's **8-10 paid visits**: the goal is realistic but tight. The website route (pay on the funnel directly) usually gives fewer, higher-intent bookings at ~$70-110 each, so ~8-12, and it can't start until the pixel is in. Watch cost per PAID booking, not cost per lead.
