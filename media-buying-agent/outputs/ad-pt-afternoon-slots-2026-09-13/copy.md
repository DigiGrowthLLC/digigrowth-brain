# Ad: Independent PT Clinic Owners — Empty Afternoon Slots

**Avatar/angle:** Independent physical therapy practice owners whose afternoon schedule has open
gaps — a narrow slice of the broader "PT practices" vertical, per the "niche down, don't go broad"
principle in `context/ad-creative-principles.md`.

**Placement:** Feed (1:1) primary; can crop to 4:5 for Reels placement later as an iteration.

---

## Version 1 (Primary)

**Hook:** "Your 2pm Slot Has Been Empty for 3 Weeks. Here's Why."

**Problem:** Most independent PT clinics don't have a marketing problem — they have a follow-up
problem. Leads come in from Google or referrals, nobody calls them back fast enough, and the
afternoon schedule stays half-empty while mornings are packed.

**Solution:** DigiGrowth runs the entire lead-gen and follow-up system for independent PT
practices — ads, automated SMS/email follow-up, and appointment reminders — so leads get booked
before they go cold, including the slots nobody else is filling.

**Proof:** We guarantee 10-20 new patient consultations within 6 weeks — or we keep working for
free until it's hit.

**CTA:** "Message us to see if your clinic qualifies."
