# CrosaCore: "Skip the Drive to PT" (AI UGC video, iteration of the winning "No Time for PT?" ad)

**Why this ad:** "Video Female UGC" / "PT That Comes to Your House" brought in all 4 lead form leads
($24 CPL, 3.33% CTR). This keeps the same actor, voice, car setting and in-home angle, and changes
only the script, so Meta treats it as an iteration of the proven ad.
**What's new:** an Austin-specific commute hook (MoPac), and "private pay" said out loud. In the first
round, 2 of 4 leads asked about insurance, so the ad now screens for that before anyone fills the form.
**Funnel stage:** top of funnel. **Format:** AI actor selfie UGC, 2 Veo 3.1 takes from `work/actor.png`, about 20s.

## Script (score 9.6/10)
| Take | Spoken (each take 8s max) |
|---|---|
| 1 | "Real question. Who has time to sit on MoPac forty minutes, twice a week, just to get to PT?" |
| 2 | "So there's a physical therapist in Austin who comes to your house. Private pay, and the first consult's free." |

Hook card: "Skip the drive to PT" · Proof card: Keating review (in-home convenience) · End card: same as the original.
Guardrails: the actor describes a situation, makes no results claim, and doesn't claim to be a patient.
There's no condition hook ("your neck pain") and no label.

## Primary text
> Hey Austin 👋 Sitting on MoPac just to get to physical therapy twice a week?
>
> Dr. Brandon Crosdale, DPT, OCS comes to your home for one-on-one PT, or you can see him at his
> Austin clinic. Private pay, superbills available for out-of-network reimbursement.
>
> Start with a free 15-minute consult.

**Headline:** Skip the Drive. PT Comes to You · alt: PT at Home in Austin
**Description:** Free 15-min consult · **Button:** Sign Up (same as the winner)
