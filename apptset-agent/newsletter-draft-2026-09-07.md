## Email Preview

Hey {{first_name}},

Confession: the "thanks for booking!" email you send today is doing almost nothing.

It gets opened once.

Then the appointment sits on the calendar — unattended — until the day it's either kept or missed.

Here's the thing.

The gap between "booked" and "showed up" is where most service businesses quietly lose money.

The old way: one confirmation email, maybe a manual reminder call if someone remembers.

That's one touchpoint carrying the entire job of getting a stranger to actually show up.

The AI Growth Engine does it differently.

Instead of one email, it runs a short automated onboarding flow: an instant confirmation, a friendly re-confirm a day or two out, and a same-day "here's what to expect" message right before the call.

No extra work on your end.

It just runs — every time someone books.

One independent service business we looked at replaced its single confirmation email with exactly this kind of sequence.

Nothing fancy — a confirmation, a re-confirm text, and a short expectation-setting message.

The pattern held: fewer no-shows, more kept appointments, without adding a single manual step to their week.

This works best for businesses that book 1:1 or small-group appointments — consultations, sessions, calls.

If that's not how you operate, skip this one.

Want me to put together a custom AI + marketing plan for {{business_name}}? Book a quick discovery call — grab a time here.

Talk soon,

Dylan | Digigrowth

P.S. — this isn't a sales pitch, just 20 minutes to see if an onboarding flow like this makes sense for {{business_name}}.
