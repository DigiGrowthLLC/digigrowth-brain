## Email Preview

Hey {{first_name}},

Most agencies want $2-5k/month before you see a single new appointment.

You can build a leaner version yourself for a fraction of that.

Here's the reframe.

The old way is either a full-service agency retainer, or a pile of disconnected tools — a chatbot here, an email sender there, a separate SMS app, none of them talking to each other.

The AI Growth Engine approach is one connected stack: lead capture, follow-up, and booking running as a single system instead of five logins.

That's the part that actually saves money — not fewer tools, fewer gaps between them.

Here's what that tends to look like in practice.

One independent service business I've talked with was paying for four separate subscriptions — a chatbot, an email tool, a texting app, a scheduler — and still losing leads in the gaps between them.

No single tool was expensive.

The overlap and the manual stitching between them were the real cost.

They collapsed it into one connected AI follow-up and booking flow.

Same leads coming in. Fewer of them going cold waiting on a reply.

The math changed because fewer leads slipped through the cracks — not because they spent more to get there.

Worth being straight about who this is for.

This works best if you're already generating some leads and just need the follow-up and booking automated.

If you don't have any lead flow yet, a lean AI stack isn't the fix — that's a different problem.

Want me to put together a custom AI + marketing plan for {{business_name}}?

Book a quick discovery call — grab a time here.

Talk soon,

Dylan | Digigrowth

P.S. — most of the cost in a DIY stack isn't the tools themselves, it's the hours spent stitching them together every week. That's usually the part worth handing off first.
