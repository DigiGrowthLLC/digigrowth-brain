## Email Preview

Hey {{first_name}},

Most "personalized" outreach is just a first-name merge tag slapped onto a generic template.

Your leads can tell.

Here's the thing—

The old way is one canned follow-up text or email, sent to everyone who fills out a form.

Same message whether someone asked about pricing, class times, or a specific goal they're working toward.

The AI Growth Engine works differently.

It reads what a lead actually said—their question, their goal, their hesitation—and **shapes the next message around that**, automatically.

Not ten versions of a template sitting in a folder.

One system that **pays attention to each lead** and responds like a person would, at a speed no person can match.

It's a pattern this space keeps proving out.

When a follow-up **references what a lead actually asked**—not just their name—more of those conversations turn into booked calls.

Because the lead feels heard, not processed.

Now—

This works best for businesses that book 1:1 sessions or consultations.

If that's not you, skip this one.

Want me to put together a **custom AI + marketing plan** for {{business_name}}?

Book a quick discovery call—grab a time here.

Talk soon,

Dylan | Digigrowth

P.S. — this isn't about replacing your voice with a bot. It's about making sure every lead gets a reply that actually fits what they asked.
