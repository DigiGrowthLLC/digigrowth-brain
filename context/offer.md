## Who it's for
Independent physical therapy practices of any payment model (insurance-based, cash-based or hybrid),
with an average plan of care worth
$1,500 or more and room for at least 7 new assessments a month.

## The problem it solves
- New-patient volume depends on physician referrals and word of mouth, so it rises and falls outside
  the owner's control. Empty treatment slots cost the same rent and payroll as full ones.
- Agencies get paid monthly retainers for activity, whether or not a single patient walks in.
- Ad inquiries arrive while the team is treating patients, and slow follow-up loses them.
- Owners rarely know their true cost per new patient or which marketing actually produces one.

## The offer
DigiGrowth books qualified, **prepaid** patients directly into the practice's evaluation schedule,
and the practice **pays per booking instead of a monthly retainer**.

The ads promote a **$49 assessment (normally $150)** with a limited number of spots each month, set
by the practice's real capacity. Patients pay the $49 when they book, which filters for intent,
raises show rates and leaves a payment record for every booking. **The practice keeps the $49.**
(For a specific practice, "normally" should be that practice's real assessment price.)

Included:
1. **Meta ad campaigns.** Creative, targeting, launch with 3-5 ad variations, weekly optimization.
2. **Booking funnel.** Landing page and checkout for the $49 assessment, built and hosted by DigiGrowth.
3. **Under-5-minute lead follow-up.** An AI SMS agent contacts every new lead, answers questions and
   books them into the practice's calendar.
4. **Reminders.** Automated confirmations and reminders before every assessment to protect show rate.
5. **Database reactivation.** The same offer sent by SMS to past patients and old leads in week one.
6. **Weekly reporting.** Spend, leads, bookings and pacing against the guarantee.

## The guarantee
**7-10 paid assessments booked in the first 30 days, with 7 guaranteed.** 7-10 is the expected
range; 7 is the guarantee. If DigiGrowth falls short of 7, it keeps running the campaigns and the
practice pays no booking fees until it has received 7.

**How to say it to prospects (website, texts, emails, pages):** "7-10 paid assessments within 30
days, guaranteed." Don't say "7 guaranteed" in customer-facing copy; the 7 floor
is the internal definition of the guarantee and goes in contracts and the call, not headlines. The practice keeps funding
the ad spend directly.

A paid assessment counts when a patient has booked and paid the $49 through the DigiGrowth checkout.
Bookings from database reactivation count toward the 7.

## KPIs (start to finish)
30 days at $30/day is $900 of ad spend ($100 of the $1,000 budget goes to software). At a $25 cost
per lead and a 17.5% booking rate that's ~36 leads and ~6 paid assessments, **one short of the 7
guaranteed**. The 7 needs 40 leads, i.e. a cost per lead of $22.50 or less, a higher booking rate,
or database reactivation bookings (which count toward the 7). 10 in 30 days needs ~57 leads, a cost
per lead near $15.75. So the week-2 checkpoint and week-one reactivation carry the guarantee.

Assumptions: 80% show rate, 35% of assessments start a plan of care worth $1,500, and $49 collected
per booking. Clinic revenue counts the $49 payments plus plan-of-care value. Plan revenue is
collected over 4-6 weeks of visits unless the practice sells prepaid packages. ROAS = clinic revenue
÷ the $1,000 marketing budget. These are projections from stated assumptions, not results; never present them
as what past clients got.

## Requirements
The guarantee applies to clinics that meet these:
- Average plan of care worth $1,500 or more (insurance-based, cash-based or hybrid all qualify).
- $1,000/month marketing budget for at least 30 days: $900 ad spend ($30/day) paid directly to Meta from
  the practice's ad account, plus $100 software.
- Capacity to see at least 7 new assessments a month.

The practice commits to:
- Answer inbound calls during business hours and honor every booked assessment.
- Give DigiGrowth access to its calendar or scheduling system.
- Report show and plan-of-care outcomes weekly so DigiGrowth can optimize lead quality.
