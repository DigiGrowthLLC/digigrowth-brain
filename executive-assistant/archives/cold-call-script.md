# Cold Call Script — Service-Based Businesses

---

"Hey is this [Name]?"

"Yes"

"Hey [Name], this is Dylan — quick question for you. I was looking at [Business Name], and I noticed [custom opener] and was wondering if that's something you guys have the room to push more of at the moment?

[Let them Answer]

Awesome, to be direct I felt this was relevant because I work with service-based businesses that utilize offers exactly like the [custom opener] you're running but feel free to cut me off if this isn't in your swimlane. I can explain the math on this one if it's relevant but we've been able to generate businesses like yours anywhere from 5-15k in new client revenue month by month, through an AI backed client acquisition system designed around offers similar to yours. So I was just reaching out to see if getting more customers in the door was something that's a genuine priority at the moment, and even worth a quick conversation."

---

"I'm Listening"

"Yea awesome not trying to pitch you anything here, just trying to see if this is something we could even set up a quick chat about, but simply how we go about introducing that amount of business for you guys is by guaranteeing 20-40 new client appointments over the next six weeks based around whatever offer you're running at the moment, and with industry average close rates of 25% and a client LTV of $1,000 to $2,000, we've easily been able to generate businesses similar to yours 5, 10, sometimes even 15k in new client revenue month by month, in a completely hands off process that didn't load them up with more work than they already had. So I just wanted to call and see if it was worth having a quick chat where I can present the process to you, worst case you feel it isn't a fit and you can run off and implement parts of it yourself."

---

"What does the process look like specifically"

"Yea so what we actually build for businesses like yours is the infrastructure and systems behind the scenes that removes you as the bottleneck — especially around new client lead handling, follow-up, and getting appointments booked directly onto your calendar."

"It's a little hard to explain properly on a quick call like this, but the goal is simple — new clients come in without everything running through you. Is it worth setting up a quick call so I can explain it to you better, talk about where you're at with the business, see if we can help, worst case you walk away knowing more ways to bring in new clients yourself."

---

"Let's do it"

"Awesome — got your calendar on you?"

*Booked*

"OK now can we just agree on a couple of things? Now, I, I don't know if I can help you — so if by the end of this meeting you don't feel comfortable with me or you don't think I'm someone you wanna move forward with, would you feel OK telling me no?"

*(yeah absolutely)*

"*(mirror)* and likewise, look, if I don't feel I can help you I'll be honest as well and say you know [Name], I can't help you — you wouldn't be upset if I said I couldn't help you despite the fact you're agreeing to meet with me?"

*(no no that's fine)*

"*(mirror)* And one last thing, if we don't say no to each other, do you mind spending a few minutes trying to figure out a clear next step on how we'd move forwards… if we get that far?"

*(of course)*

"*(mirror)* well look I'll send you an email confirming this at [email] — that's right isn't it?"

*(yes)*

"Alright I'll get an invite over to you. Oh and one last question — can you think of any reason as to why you'd want to cancel? Not gonna panic about speaking with a guy you meet off of a cold call are you?"

*(laughs, no I'll be there)*

## Opener Examples (for [custom opener] field)

- "a new client welcome offer"
- "a complimentary first consultation"
- "a new customer special you're running"
- "a first-visit discount for local customers"

## Key Numbers

- Deliverable: 20–40 new client appointments in 6 weeks
- Avg visit value: ~$200
- Client LTV: $1,000–$2,000
- Revenue range to quote: 5–15k/month
- Mechanism: Done-for-you Meta ads running a complimentary intro offer — customers opt in, we book them directly onto the calendar

## Qualifying Signals (before calling)

- Has a website but no visible ad presence
- Google reviews but no recent posting activity
- Facebook page inactive 30+ days
- No corporate/franchise affiliation
- Not already at capacity
