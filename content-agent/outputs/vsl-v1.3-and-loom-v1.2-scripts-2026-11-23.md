## VSL V.1.3 (website Contact page video)

Source: the live 1028-pt-pitch-v5 recording (VSL V.1.2). **Change: the pain section only**
(audit item 6). The VSL's pain was physician referrals drying up; only 1 of DigiGrowth's 4 buyers
(Pinnacle) gets patients from physicians. The others named word of mouth, events, Instagram, and
ads that stopped working. Every sentence in the new section is a P1-P4 finding in prospects' own
words.

> If you own an independent physical therapy practice, I'll guarantee you 7-10 new prepaid
> assessments on your calendar in the next 30 days. Free. Not leads, not clicks. Booked prepaid
> assessments. In exchange for one thing: if it works, I get to use it as a case study.
>
> Now I don't work with law firms, med spas, or healthcare in general, just independent
> owner-operated PT practices. I'm taking on a small number at no cost right now to build
> documented results before I raise my rates.
>
> **[NEW, replaces "Now here's the problem most practices have... other people's decisions."]**
> **Now here's what I hear from almost every owner I talk to. New patients are hit or miss. Word of
> mouth is great until it slows down. You've probably tried ads, and they either flopped, or worked
> for a while and just stopped. The leads you did get wanted to use their insurance. And you're
> treating all day, texting back missed calls between patients. Some months you're full, some
> months there are open slots on a Wednesday afternoon, and nothing changed on your end. That's not
> on you. It just means you don't have the proper systems in place.**
>
> What I run is specifically built for PT practices. How you get found, how fast you follow up, how
> a schedule gets built and stays that way. In 30 days, qualified interest starts coming in,
> everyone gets followed up entirely on your behalf, and you end up with 7-10 prepaid assessments
> without having to lift a finger.
>
> You pay us nothing to find out, nothing after, whether it works or not. Apply below for one of
> the free pilot slots. It's a short screening call to check fit, not a sales pitch. Spots are
> limited and won't stay open, so I encourage you to book in as soon as possible. Thank you for your
> time. I look forward to speaking with you soon.

**Not changed, but flagged (audit item 4, not selected):** "You pay us nothing to find out,
nothing after, whether it works or not" contradicts offer.md (the practice funds a $1,000/month
marketing budget, then pays per booking after the pilot). If you re-record the pain section anyway,
this is the moment to swap it for: "For the pilot, our fees are waived. The only cost is the ad
budget, paid straight to Meta. If we don't hit it, we keep working, and you don't pay us a cent more
until we do."
