# Email Sequence Skill

Write cold outreach and nurture email sequences for DigiGrowth.

---

## Trigger

Any request containing: "email", "sequence", "cold outreach", "follow-up email", "nurture", "drip"

---

## Mode 1: Cold Outreach Sequence (5-Email)

**Target:** Independent service-based business owners who haven't heard from DigiGrowth (specify the vertical for the campaign — see `context/brand.md`)

**Sequence structure:**

| # | Type | Subject line approach | Length |
|---|------|----------------------|--------|
| 1 | Intro + value | Specific to their business | 3–4 sentences |
| 2 | Follow-up | Pain-point question | 2–3 sentences |
| 3 | Social proof / case study | Result-led subject | 3–5 sentences |
| 4 | Objection handler | Address the #1 objection | 3–4 sentences |
| 5 | Break-up | Direct, low-pressure close | 2–3 sentences |

**Email 1 formula:**
- Open: reference something specific (their location, business name, vertical)
- One sentence on what DigiGrowth does and the specific result
- Ask: "Would it make sense to show you how this works for [type of business]?"
- Sign-off: first name only

**Rules:**
- No buzzwords ("synergy", "leverage", "game-changer")
- No walls of text — short paragraphs, white space
- Each email stands alone — don't assume they read the last one
- Always end with a low-friction CTA (question, not demand)

---

## Mode 2: Nurture Sequence (post-lead-magnet or post-opt-in)

**Target:** Business owners who showed interest but haven't booked

**Sequence:** 7 emails over 14 days
- Days 1, 3, 5: Value emails (tips, insights, frameworks)
- Days 7, 10: Case study / proof emails
- Days 12, 14: Direct offer emails

**Value email topics (adapt to the target vertical):**
- "Why most [vertical] ads fail in the first 30 days"
- "The 3-part follow-up system that books more consultations"
- "How to turn [platform] traffic into booked appointments"

---

## Mode 3: Single Email (one-off)

**Use when:** Dylan needs one specific email — outreach, follow-up, or announcement

**Process:**
1. Ask: Who is the recipient? What's the goal? Any context on their business?
2. Write the email
3. Offer a subject line (and 1–2 alternatives)
4. Ask if they want a follow-up drafted too

---

## After Writing

1. Save to `outputs/email-[sequence-name].md`
2. Include subject lines and send-day schedule in the file header
3. Offer to personalize for a specific prospect if names/details are provided
