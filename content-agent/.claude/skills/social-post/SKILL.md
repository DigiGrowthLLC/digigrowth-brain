# Social Post Skill

Generate platform-optimized social media posts for DigiGrowth or Dylan's personal brand.

---

## Trigger

Any request containing: "post", "LinkedIn", "Instagram", "tweet", "X post", "social", "caption"

---

## Mode 1: LinkedIn Post

**Use when:** Dylan asks for a LinkedIn post, thought leadership content, or personal brand content.

**Format:**
- Line 1: Hook — short, curiosity-driven, bold claim, or pattern interrupt. No emoji.
- Blank line
- Body: 3–6 short paragraphs or a list. Each paragraph max 2 sentences.
- Blank line
- CTA or closing thought
- 3–5 hashtags on final line

**Hook formulas:**
- "Most [target] are doing [thing] wrong."
- "I [did thing]. Here's what happened."
- "[Contrarian claim about industry]"
- "The difference between [A] and [B] is [unexpected thing]."

**Lengths:** Short (150–250 words) or Medium (300–500 words). Ask if not specified.

**Output:** Write 2 versions (A/B). Ask Dylan which direction to develop further.

---

## Mode 2: Instagram Caption

**Use when:** Dylan asks for an Instagram caption or post.

**Format:**
- Hook line (same rules as LinkedIn)
- 2–4 short paragraphs (conversational, punchy)
- CTA line ("DM me 'GROW' to learn more" style)
- Blank line
- 10–15 hashtags in a block

**Note:** Instagram rewards authenticity and specificity. Avoid generic fitness content — focus on the business-owner angle.

---

## Mode 3: X (Twitter) Post

**Use when:** Dylan asks for a tweet or X post.

**Format:**
- Single tweet: max 280 chars. Lead with the best line.
- Thread: number each post (1/, 2/, etc.). Hook tweet first, value in middle, CTA last.

---

## After Writing

1. Ask: "Want me to save this to `outputs/`?"
2. If yes: save as `outputs/[platform]-post-YYYY-MM-DD.md`
3. Offer to write a second variation or adapt for another platform
