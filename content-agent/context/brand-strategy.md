# Personal Brand Strategy

Reference knowledge for building Dylan's personal brand and creating content. Distilled from study of brand-building principles.

---

## What a Brand Actually Is

- A brand is an **association between two things** — you and a topic, outcome, or feeling
- Branding is just teaching people to associate you with something valuable
- **3 elements of brand strength:**
  1. **Influence** — does seeing your name/face change people's behavior?
  2. **Directionality** — does it move them towards you or away?
  3. **Reach** — how many people have that association with you?
- You are always the purest version of your brand — lean into more of you, not less
- Be careful who you appear next to — audiences associate you with whoever shares your stage

---

## Organic Content Strategy

- **Early stage:** content = lead nurture. Don't worry about views. As audience grows, it becomes acquisition.
- **No CTAs inside content** — they kill reach and feel off. Put CTAs in: description, link in bio, and paid ads only.
- **Give:Ask separation** — content is ALL give. Ads are ALL ask. Never try to balance a give/ask ratio inside a single piece of content.
- **3:1 feed ratio** — for every ad someone sees from you, they should have seen 3 pieces of free value content first
- Paid ads protect the brand — they serve as the CTA vehicle while content stays pure

## Content Structure — The 3 P's Hook Framework

Every piece of content (video, post, article) should open with:

1. **Promise** — what will they get from this? What's the outcome?
2. **Proof** — why should they listen to you specifically? Establish credibility fast.
3. **Path** — show there's structure. Set expectations, then meet them.

The first 30 seconds are everything. Match the headline/thumbnail exactly with your opening words — if it says "5 ways to get clients," confirm that in the first sentence.

---

## Content Formats — Only 3 Exist

All content is some combination of:
- **Lists** — numbered or grouped points
- **Steps** — sequential process
- **Stories** — narrative examples and case studies

These can be intermixed freely (a list of steps, each with a story). Short-form = one of these. Long-form = many of these strung together.

---

## Topic Generation — 4 Buckets

Never run out of content by rotating between:

1. **Real-time** — capture ideas as they happen (tweet it, phone note, voice memo). These become everything else.
2. **Recent past** — review your calendar weekly. Every meeting, problem, or client situation is content.
3. **Far past** — big lessons, belief shifts, pivotal decisions that changed how you operate.
4. **Manufactured** — intentional challenges or experiments ("I did X for 30 days", "I started from scratch").

Build a repository where ideas live. Tweets → captions, scripts, reels, emails, posts. One idea, many formats.

## Volume vs. Quality

- Start with **quantity** to learn what works — you cannot know quality without reps
- **Power law:** 10x more time on one piece doesn't get 10x results — it can get 100x results
- As you scale, shift toward fewer, higher-quality pieces
- **Niche ratio:** 3:1 or 4:1 niche content to broad/lifestyle content — niche content gets actual customers, broad content expands reach

---

## Algorithm — Always Align With the Platform

- Every platform's objective: **keep people on the platform**
- They optimize for: **CTR → watch-through rate → watch-another rate**
- Hook drives CTR → completion drives distribution → rewatching drives recommendations
- Never rely on hacks (hashtag tricks, posting time tricks, etc.) — all platforms eventually close the loop
- Long-term strategy: make content people click on, watch all the way through, and immediately watch more of

---

## Framework Creation Process

When you have a repeatable insight worth packaging:

1. **Consume a large dataset** — watch your top content, interview customers, review your own results
2. **List everything** you observe across examples
3. **Find the commonalities** — what do they ALL have?
4. **Strip to essentials** — usually 3-5 things
5. **Make it memorable** — alliteration, acronym, or rhyme (Promise → Proof → Path)

---

## Scaling Content Production

- Start with an agency to buy lessons and stay accountable — speed > perfection early
- Transition in-house when your team's output quality beats the agency's
- Tweets/notes are the source of everything — they feed captions, scripts, posts, and reels
- One idea → tweet → caption → reel → email → blog = compounding leverage

---

## Controversy

- Controversy increases **reach only** — it doesn't improve influence strength or directionality
- Treat it the same as trending topics: useful for CTR, not a brand-building strategy
- If some people are turned away, that's fine — reach more people and provide more value to compensate
