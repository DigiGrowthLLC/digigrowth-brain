# SEO Keyword & Content Pillar Map

Reference for the `seo-blog` skill and any long-form/authority content. Keeps blog output aimed at
real search intent instead of generic marketing topics. Update as posts are published and search
performance data comes in (see `memory.md`).

## Content Pillars

Each pillar maps to a cluster of buyer questions/objections service-based business owners actually
search. Every blog post should belong to one pillar and target one primary keyword + 2-3 related
long-tail variants.

### 1. Lead Follow-Up & Automation
- "lead follow-up automation for small business"
- "how fast should you respond to a new lead"
- "why leads go cold before you call them back"
- "SMS follow-up for [vertical] businesses"

### 2. Paid Ads That Actually Convert
- "why isn't my Facebook/Meta ads campaign converting"
- "how much should I spend on ads for a service business"
- "Meta ads for [vertical] businesses that actually work"
- "signs your marketing agency is wasting your ad spend"

### 3. Client Acquisition Systems (bottom-funnel, high intent)
- "how to get more clients for [service business]"
- "done-for-you client acquisition for small business"
- "guaranteed leads for [vertical] businesses"
- "how much should I pay for a marketing agency"

### 4. Objection / Trust Content (mirrors brand.md objections)
- "I tried ads before and they didn't work — what to check first"
- "how to vet a marketing agency before signing a contract"
- "questions to ask before hiring a lead gen agency"

## Vertical Rotation

Fitness is the current proof-point niche (real case studies live here first). Keep a 3:1 ratio of
vertical-agnostic pillar content to fitness-specific content so the blog reads as a service-business
resource, not a fitness-only blog — per the broadened target client in `brand.md`.

## Cadence

Start with 2-4 cornerstone posts (one per pillar above) before scaling to a regular cadence. Thin or
duplicate content hurts a brand-new domain more than it helps — depth over volume until the first
posts show traction.

## Tracking

Log published posts and any ranking/traffic signal in `memory.md` under a new "SEO Content" section —
same pattern as the existing hook-tracking log.
