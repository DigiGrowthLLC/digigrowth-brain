NICHE DEFINITION:
Qualified = privately owned facility where 1-on-1 or semi-private personal training is the PRIMARY service or a core revenue model. Sports performance facilities with private ownership also qualify.

Disqualified = big box gyms, franchise locations, group-only studios (yoga-only, cycling-only, Pilates-only, dance-only), or any business where personal training is an add-on rather than the focus.

COLD CALL OPENER CRITERIA:
The one-line opener feeds into this exact cold call framework:
Hey [Name], this is Dylan — quick question for you. I was looking at [Studio Name], and I noticed you're running [offer their running'] and was wondering how many people are you putting through that each month?

The opener must complete that sentence naturally. It should sound like something Dylan observed that is genuinely interesting or specific — not a compliment.

Priority order for what to pull from the website:
1. A specific RESULT or outcome they advertise (e.g., "your 6-week transformation challenge", "the 30-day guarantee you're running", "those before/after results on your homepage")
2. A specific OFFER or program name (e.g., "that semi-private small group model", "your unlimited membership at that price point", "the nutrition coaching bundle")
3. A specific NICHE or client type they serve (e.g., "working exclusively with women over 40", "the athlete performance track", "that postpartum fitness program")
4. A specific METHOD or system (e.g., "the 3-day strength template", "that hybrid in-person virtual setup", "the assessment-first intake process")
5. If nothing specific exists — return null. Do not fabricate.

OPENER TONE RULES:
- Always sounds like Dylan is calling because he noticed something specific, not because he's running a list
- Never complimentary — observational only
- Ends with a curiosity question when possible to get the prospect talking
- Short, punchy, conversational — not a monologue
The opener must:
- Be 15 words or fewer
- Sound casual and observational, not like a sales pitch or compliment
- Reference something real and specific — never generic like "your great reviews" or "your beautiful facility"
- Set up the pivot to intro sessions and consults naturally
- If a result or dollar figure is mentioned on their site, use it
- If a challenge, guarantee, or transformation program exists, reference it
- Never mention the website, never say "I was browsing" or "I came across"
