# Cold Calling Script V.1.0 — Physical Therapy Clinics

**ICP:** Owner/lead PT at an independent physical therapy clinic (1-3 locations, not a large chain).
**Pain points assumed:** referral-dependent patient flow (relies on doctor referrals, which fluctuate), inconsistent new-patient volume between referral cycles, owner is a treating clinician first and marketer never, no-show/cancellation rate eats capacity, competing against big PT chains and med-spa-style "instant gratification" recovery options.
**Offer:** AI-backed client acquisition system generating direct-to-consumer new-patient consultations, independent of doctor referral flow, on a pay-by-performance basis.

*Numbers below are placeholders — swap in real figures (LTV, close rate, results range) before using live.*

---

## 1. Opener (permission-based, disarming)

> "Hey, is this [Name]? ... Awesome — it's Dylan, I'll be honest, this is a bit out of left field. Do you mind if I take 30 seconds, and you can tell me if it's relevant or not?"

*[Let them answer]*

> "Great — feel free to cut me off if this isn't in your lane. Quick question though: are you still the one handling new patient growth at [Clinic Name], or has that shifted to someone else?"

**Levers used:** politeness bias (asking permission), curiosity tendency (no reveal yet), permission-based framing.

## 2. Credibility / value frame with concrete numbers

> "Cool — so the reason I'm calling is I've talked to a lot of PT clinic owners lately, and the same thing keeps coming up: most of your new patients come from doctor referrals, which is great until referral flow slows down and there's not much you can do about it in the meantime. Is that something you deal with?"

*[Let them respond]*

> "Yeah, figured — and most clinics I talk to feel like they should be filling that gap with direct-to-consumer patients, but between treating a full schedule and running the clinic, there's just no time left to actually build that pipeline. So what we've built is a system that brings clinics like yours new-patient consultations directly — not through referrals — on a pay-by-performance basis, meaning if it doesn't produce, you don't pay. Clinics we've worked with have added anywhere from 15-30 new consultations a month running alongside their existing referral flow. So I wasn't calling to sell you anything today, just wanted to see if filling that gap between referral cycles is actually a priority right now, or not really on your radar."

**Levers used:** subjective validation (naming the referral-dependency pain), zero risk bias (pay-by-performance), tried n' tested bias ("clinics we've worked with"), disarming honesty ("wasn't calling to sell you anything").

## 3. Curiosity-gap process explanation

If they ask "how does it work":

> "Yeah so it's a little hard to do justice on a quick call like this, but at a high level — we build out the systems that bring in and follow up with new-patient leads automatically, so it's not sitting on your front desk's plate on top of everything else they're already doing. The short version is: it runs in the background, and you just show up to full booked slots. Worth a quick call where I walk you through it properly, see where you're at with growth right now, and worst case you leave with a few ideas you can run yourself even if it's not a fit?"

**Levers used:** curiosity tendency (deliberately incomplete), Result-not-Process pillar, zero-pressure framing.

## 4. Booking ask

> "Sound worth 15 minutes to at least see if it's a fit?"

*[Yes]*

> "Awesome — got your calendar in front of you?"

*[Book on the spot]*

## 5. Pre-frame / Ben-Franklin-style close

> "Perfect, one last thing before I let you go — can we just agree on a couple things? I don't know yet if we can actually help you, so if by the end of that call it's not feeling like a fit, would you be comfortable just telling me no?"

*(Yeah, of course)*

> "Same on my end — if I don't think we can genuinely help grow the clinic, I'll tell you straight, even if you're ready to move forward. Fair?"

*(Yeah that's fine)*

> "Last thing — assuming neither of us says no, would you be open to spending a few minutes figuring out a clear next step, if it gets there?"

*(Sure)*

> "Great, I'll send a confirmation to [email] — that the best one? ... Perfect, you'll get an invite shortly. And no reason you'd need to cancel on me, right — not gonna get nervous talking to a guy from a cold call?"

*(Laughs, no I'll be there)*

## Objection Handling (Agree → Reframe → Redirect)

**"We already get referrals from doctors, we're fine."**
> "Totally get that, and that's honestly the best kind of patient to have — my question is more: is that referral flow consistent month to month, or does it swing up and down depending on the season/relationships? [If it swings] That's exactly the gap we fill — not replacing referrals, just making sure you're not fully dependent on them."

**"We're already booked / at capacity."**
> "That's a good problem to have — is that true max capacity, or more like a comfortable ceiling you could flex past if the right patients showed up? A lot of clinics that feel 'full' still have a no-show/cancellation gap that's costing them more than they think."

**"Send me an email."**
> "Happy to — what specifically would you want in it so I make sure it's actually useful and not just another thing in your inbox? Honestly easier if I just give you the 30-second version now instead of playing email tag for two weeks."

**"Not interested."**
> "No worries — can I ask, is that because growing beyond referrals isn't something you need right now, or more that you're not sure a random call is the way to find someone to help with that?"

**"We tried marketing before, didn't work."**
> "Yeah, that's really common actually — what did you try, ads or something else? [Listen] Got it — most of what doesn't work for clinics is generic lead gen that isn't set up for how PT patients actually decide. Worth a quick look at what specifically didn't work before we assume this would go the same way?"
