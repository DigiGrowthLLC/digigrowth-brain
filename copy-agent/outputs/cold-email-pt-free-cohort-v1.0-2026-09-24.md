resolve a custom opener or a Loom). Placeholders: `{{first_name}}`, `{{company}}`, `[CUSTOM OPENER]`,
`[LOOM LINK]`. Rename the `{{ }}` ones to match your sending tool's variable syntax.

### How to write the [CUSTOM OPENER] so it flows
- 1-2 sentences, 15-30 words, ends with a period. No greeting (the "Hi" is already there) and no pitch.
- Name one real, specific thing, then add a short human reaction to it.
- The next line starts "Reason I'm reaching out:", so the opener must not already state a reason.

Examples:
- "Saw you just opened a second clinic in Round Rock. Growing that fast in a crowded market is no small thing."
- "Read the review from the patient who got back to running after her ACL surgery. You can tell your team really cares about outcomes."
- "Noticed you do in-home visits across all of North Austin. That's a lot of windshield time, but patients clearly love the convenience."

## Self-check
- Word counts: T1 inside 50-125 once the opener is added; follow-ups inside 30-80.
- Links: one (Loom) in Touch 1 only. Touches 2-3 have none, which helps deliverability.
- "Free" never appears as a word: it's "no management fee" and "at no cost", once each. That keeps spam filters quiet.
- No borrowed proof, no invented stats, and no "this isn't automated" claims.
- The guarantee matches the offer file. The ad spend is disclosed in Touch 2, so "no fee" never reads as "costs nothing."
- Each touch makes sense on its own, has one call to action, and gets softer as the sequence goes.

## Before sending, confirm
- **"3 practices this month" / "room for one or two more"** — only keep these if the limit is real right now. The offer file set 3 per cycle in July.
- **Loom wording** — "I recorded a quick video" is accurate whether it's one general video or one per prospect. Only say "for you" if each prospect gets their own.
