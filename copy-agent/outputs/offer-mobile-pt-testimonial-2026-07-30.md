# Offer: Mobile/In-Home PT Case Study Cohort (Testimonial Phase)

**Context:** DigiGrowth's paid offer is $1,500/mo for 10-20 booked intro sessions (Meta ads +
automations + SMS/email). This is a free-labor variant to generate testimonials/case studies in the
mobile/in-home physical therapy niche. The only PT cost is a $500 ad spend commitment, paid directly
to Meta (not to DigiGrowth). Positioning goal: authority/selectivity, not "please let me work for
you free."

---

## Value Equation Check

- **Dream Outcome ↑** — framed as "10-20 booked consultations," not "marketing services"
- **Likelihood of Achievement ↑** — driven by the guarantee + "you pay the platform, not me" trust move
- **Time Delay ↓** — 30-day sprint, results tracked live
- **Effort & Sacrifice ↓** — PT does nothing but fund ad spend and show up to booked sessions; DigiGrowth runs everything

---

## Dream Outcome

"A full week of new-patient consultations booked onto your calendar — without touching ad accounts,
chasing leads, or hiring anyone."

Not "marketing services." Not "social media management." The transformation is a booked calendar.

---

## Problems → Solutions → Delivery

| Phase | Problem | Solution | Delivery Vehicle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before | Skeptical ads work for PT specifically (not a med spa or gym) | Niche-specific case studies + this program's own results become the proof | Done-for-you, no dashboard access required to start |
| Before | Doesn't want to hand $1,500/mo to an unproven agency | Zero agency fee during the cohort — only pays the ad platform directly | Client keeps their own Meta ad account, DigiGrowth manages inside it |
| During | No time to run or monitor campaigns | Full campaign build + management (audience, creative, budget pacing) | Done-for-you |
| During | Doesn't want to field unqualified callers | Leads are qualified and pre-booked before they hit the PT's calendar | Automated SMS/email qualification + booking flow |
| After | Leads go cold without follow-up | Automated nurture sequence for no-shows/non-responders | Done-for-you automation, no manual follow-up needed |

---

## Bonuses (each with stated value, stacked on top of the free core service)

1. **Lead Qualification Layer** — every lead is filtered before it reaches the practice, so no time
   wasted on tire-kickers. *(Normally part of the $1,500/mo package — value: $500/mo)*
2. **Automated No-Show Recovery Sequence** — SMS/email flow that re-books no-shows automatically.
   *(Value: $500 standalone)*
3. **Live Lead Dashboard** — real-time visibility into every lead and booking status, no waiting on a
   weekly report. *(Value: $300/mo)*
4. **No Contract** — cancel anytime, no term commitment beyond the 30-day sprint.

Stacked bonus value: **~$1,300**, on top of the $1,500/mo core service — all included at $0 agency
fee for the cohort.

---

## Guarantee (risk reversal)

**Performance guarantee, not money-back** (there's no fee to refund — the risk is their time and the
$500 ad spend):

> "If we don't book at least 10 qualified consultations onto your calendar in the first 30 days, we
> keep managing your account free for a second 30 days — at no additional cost — until we hit that
> number."

This covers their actual risk (wasted ad spend with nothing to show for it), not just a fee they
never paid.

---

## Scarcity / Urgency

Real, not manufactured: DigiGrowth is only running this cohort with **3 mobile/in-home PT
practices** this cycle — enough to service well and produce strong case studies, not so many it
looks like a mass giveaway. State a close date or "first 3 that qualify" framing.

---

## Reason Why (the authority move — this is what keeps it from reading as begging)

State it plainly, don't dodge it:

> "I'm building out a small case-study cohort specifically in the mobile/in-home PT space before
> opening this up more broadly. For a short window, qualifying practices get the full $1,500/mo
> service at no agency fee — you only fund the ad spend itself, paid directly to Meta, the same as
> if you ran the ads yourself. In exchange, I ask for a testimonial and the right to reference the
> results once we hit them."

This does three things: explains why free is on the table (cohort-building, not desperation), keeps
money flowing to the platform not to Dylan (removes "is this a scam" doubt), and states the ask
(testimonial) as a fair trade rather than hiding it.

---

## Qualification Criteria ("if they qualify" — this is the exclusivity lever)

Only offer this to a practice that:
- Is an independent mobile/in-home physical therapy practice (fits the proven ICP)
- Has capacity to actually handle 10-20 new consultations/month without turning patients away
- Can commit $500 in ad spend, paid directly to Meta
- Has (or will set up) a working intake/booking process to receive the leads
- Agrees to a testimonial/case study once results are hit

Framing the qualification criteria out loud in outreach reinforces authority — you're vetting them,
not the other way around.

## Stress Test

- **Value Equation:** raises perceived likelihood (guarantee + "pay the platform not me") and drops
  effort/sacrifice (they do nothing but fund spend + show up), while keeping the dream outcome
  concrete (10-20 consultations, 30 days).
- **Skeptical buyer:** the "you pay Meta directly" detail and stated qualification bar are the two
  things doing the most work to kill "is this a scam" doubt.
- **Authority, not begging:** the ask (testimonial) is stated as a trade, not hidden; scarcity (3
  spots) and qualification criteria put DigiGrowth in the selective seat, not the PT.
