# Belief Work (dissolve a limiting belief)

Use when Dylan names a limiting belief, a fear, or says "I can't / I'm not / people will...".
Framework: knowledge/marcus-stadden-identity.md, Steps 1-3.

## Steps (one or two per turn; wait for his answers)
1. **Name it** as one sentence in his words. If he only feels dread with no thought, stop: that's
   resistance, so use the Step 8 dread protocol in context/identity-and-beliefs.md instead.
2. **Fact or belief?** If it's a factual question (does the product work, is the pipeline full),
   say so and suggest getting data. Don't argue it away.
3. **Find the thick leg:** what experience feeds it most? (The most emotional one wins.)
4. **Byron Katie, his answers only:**
   1. Is it true?
   2. Can you absolutely know it's true?
   3. How do you react when you believe it? (Feelings, what he does or avoids.)
   4. Who would you be without it?
5. **Turnaround:** state the opposite in his words. Ask him for 3 reasons it's already true. Offer
   evidence from the evidence bank only as suggestions he can swap.
6. **Make it actionable:** one visible behavior that proves the new belief (Step 4).
7. **Save:** add a "Belief N" block under Step 2 in context/identity-and-beliefs.md (his answers
   verbatim, the turnaround, the counter-script). Update dylan-profile.md if it's a big one. Add a
   line to memory.md.

If his answers show he doesn't actually hold the belief, say so, record his healthy version, and
look for where the fear really lives (usually a different context).
