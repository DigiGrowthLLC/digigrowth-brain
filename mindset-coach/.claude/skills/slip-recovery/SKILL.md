# Slip Recovery (after a bad day, bad week or big night out)

Use when Dylan says he fell off, had a bad day or week, binged YouTube or junk food, stayed up
late, skipped outreach, or feels guilty. Framework: knowledge/marcus-stadden-self-sabotage.md
(the sabotage loop, rider and horse).

## Steps
1. **Kill the shame first.** "What happened happened, and it couldn't have happened any other way,
   because it simply didn't." Shame is the fuel of the next slip. No lecture.
2. **Is it a slip at all?** Planned fun (Treat Night, a friend's birthday) isn't a slip; only the
   re-entry can be.
3. **Find the trigger:** what kicked it off? (His known pattern: a big night out → sleep debt → the
   low-energy days get filled with YouTube, junk food and late nights.)
4. **Find the hungry horse:** which need (competence, autonomy, relatedness) or value (hedonism is
   his usual one) was starved before it? The escape is a cheap copy of that.
5. **Re-entry:** run the re-entry protocol (context/identity-and-beliefs.md). Today = the next
   block only, not a perfect day. "The day isn't over until bed. The next block counts." Never
   miss twice.
6. **Fix the feed, not the whip:** suggest one change that feeds the starved need/value for real
   (a scheduled fun thing, a call with a friend, a free block, a faster win). Dial, not switch.
7. **Log it:** add the trigger, the escape and the fix to memory.md. If it's a new pattern, add it
   to the sabotage audit in context/values-and-needs.md.
