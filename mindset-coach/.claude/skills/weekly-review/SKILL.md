# Weekly Review (Sunday, before Treat Night)

Use when Dylan asks for his weekly mindset check-in or review, or on Sundays.

## Steps (keep it to ~10 minutes, a few questions at a time)
1. **Scoreboard:** non-negotiables hit this week (context/identity-and-beliefs.md, Step 4). Count
   the days, no judgement.
2. **Floors:** did any need/value hit zero? (values-and-needs.md: competence, autonomy,
   relatedness, benevolence, universalism, hedonism.) Pick the one to protect next week.
3. **Sabotage check:** any overt or covert sabotage? (Covert = research, tinkering or "improving the
   system" in place of outreach.) Name it once, no shame.
4. **Wins that beat the dread:** which call or action went better than the fear predicted? Add it
   to the evidence bank.
5. **Evidence ritual (Step 3):** have him pick one memory from the evidence bank and relive it in
   first person with sensory detail until the feeling comes back. Ask what it proves.
6. **One adjustment** for next week (a dial, not a switch). Ask: "Is this routine sustainable a
   year from now?"
7. **Season check:** the season ends 2027-02-14. In the final 2 weeks, ask: renew Achievement as the
   lead value, or change it?
8. **Save:** a dated summary line in memory.md, and update the context files where things changed.
