## Step 2: Byron Katie
**Belief 1: "Rejection means I failed / I'm not good enough." (done 2026-11-17)**
Thickest table leg = Advantage Therapy not renewing.
1. True? "The service wasn't good enough for them yet. That reflects my experience level."
2. Absolutely know? "No. He may also just have been too busy (his text). Probably both."
3. Reaction? "Keep improving the service."
4. Without it? "I see rejection as a good thing, a way to improve."
**Result:** he doesn't hold this belief about client churn. He separates the service from himself.
His empowering version is already there: *"A no is feedback on the service, not a verdict on me."*
The fear is more likely in cold outreach (strangers, being "a bother", ridicule). That's next.

**Cold outreach (2026-11-17):** no conscious thought ("I know it's irrational"), just **dread and
not wanting to do it**. So it's resistance (an emotion), not a belief to argue with. The tool is
Step 8 (disarm the old self), not Byron Katie. Outreach paused for service delivery plus a bad
week, then **resumed 2026-11-16**: evidence that "never miss twice" works.

**Bad week (before 2026-11-16):** fell back on all three: YouTube, junk food, staying up late.
Belief 3: "I love cheap dopamine too much to prioritise what's good for me." (done 2026-11-17)
1. True? No. 2. Absolutely know? No. "Consciously I don't care for it much, just subconsciously."
3. Reaction? Sometimes spirals into **"the day is ruined"** (the day, not usually the week).
4. Without it? "Probably a better version of myself."
**Turnaround:** *"I prioritise what's good for me. A slip is one block, not a ruined day."*
Counter-script for the spiral: "The day isn't over until I'm in bed. The next block counts."
"Subconsciously" = the horse in the rider/horse metaphor: feed it (Treat Night, nights out with
friends, a daily free block) instead of whipping it with discipline.
**Trigger:** a weekend out for a friend's birthday. The weekend itself is good: it feeds Relatedness,
Benevolence and Hedonism, the starved parts in values-and-needs.md. The leak is the **re-entry**:
a sleep debt, then the low-energy days filled with YouTube, junk food and late nights.

### Re-entry protocol after a big night/weekend out (PROPOSED)
1. **Plan the night as a win, not a slip.** No guilt; shame is what turns one night into a week.
2. **Day after = recovery day, decided in advance:** no alarm-guilt, water and real food, a walk
   or light gym, one 15-min work task. It doesn't count as a miss.
3. **Day 2 = normal day at the normal start time.** This is the "never miss twice" line.
4. **Sleep reset night:** bedtime back to normal that night, phone charges outside the bedroom,
   no YouTube in bed.
5. **If day 2 slips:** shrink the day (the first block only), don't write off the week.

### Pre-outreach dread protocol (Step 8)
Run it at the start of every Cold SMS & Email / Cold Calling block, under 60 seconds:
1. **Say it out loud:** "I feel dread about dialing. It's just a feeling."
2. **Notice it:** where it sits in the body. One breath, don't fight it.
3. **Accept it:** "It can stay while I work."
4. **Get back to the work:** the first dial or send within 60 seconds. The first action is the only
   hard one.
Then **log the reference:** after the block, write one line about the call that went better than
the dread predicted (Step 7: emotion + repetition makes a reference stick).
