## Treat Night (Hedonism floor, Dylan's focus value, 2026-11-16)

**LIVE:** every **Sunday 7-10 PM** (Dylan moved it from Saturday on 2026-11-16; Sunday bedtime 11:00 PM,
eating done by 8:00 PM), recurring Google Calendar event "Treat Night" (from 2026-11-22, 3 PM
reminder to pick the meal + movie). **Use it or lose it:** if anything else fills the block
(friends, work, plans), that week's Treat Night is forfeited; it is never moved or made up. Full rules = OS SOP #74 (SOPs → Lifestyle → Treat Night),
which is the source of truth; it's the one planned exception to the Media Consumption + Diet
Protocols. Review Sunday 2026-12-06.

Dylan's worry: one junk-food + movie night spirals into bad sleep, "bed rotting" and a lost next
day. The system is built to stop that:

1. **Planned, same night every week.** It's the plan working, not a slip. No guilt, because guilt
   (shame) is what restarts the sabotage loop.
2. **Decided in advance:** the meal and the movie are picked earlier that day. One meal, not
   grazing all night; one movie, not scrolling for one.
3. **Bounded:** starts ~7:00 PM; eating done by ~8:30 PM (a late heavy meal wrecks sleep); no
   caffeine/soda after 4 PM.
4. **Couch or TV, not bed.** The bed stays for sleep, so it never turns into bed rotting.
5. **The credits are the finish line.** No YouTube or autoplay after; YouTube isn't part of the
   treat, it's the slide. Shutdown: plate away → teeth → phone charges outside the bedroom →
   lights out within 30 min of his normal time.
6. **Next-morning anchor:** a fixed, easy first block at the normal start time (walk or gym + one
   15-min task). Rule: **never miss twice.** A sloppy night is fine; a sloppy next day is what we
   prevent.
7. **Dial, not switch:** if it spills over, shrink it (earlier end, movie only), don't cancel it.
   Review after 3 weeks (~2026-12-07).
