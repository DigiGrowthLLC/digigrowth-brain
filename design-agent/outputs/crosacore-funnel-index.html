Common Questions

Before you schedule

Is this covered by insurance?

CrosaCore is cash-pay and does not bill insurance directly. Superbills are available so you can seek out-of-network reimbursement on your own. The consultation itself is free either way, so there's no cost to find out if this makes sense for you.

I've already tried physical therapy. Why would this be different?

Previous care may have genuinely helped, while another part of the job remained unfinished. A diagnosis may not explain the entire experience, and symptom relief doesn't always rebuild capacity. The Rebuild Method is built specifically to close that gap, not to repeat what you've already tried.

What actually happens on the call?

A focused, 15-minute phone conversation to understand your main concern, clarify whether CrosaCore is a fit, and identify the right next step, including a referral elsewhere if that's the honest answer.

Is there any pressure to commit to anything on the call?

No. The consultation is a conversation, not a sales pitch, and no same-day decision is ever required. If CrosaCore isn't the right fit, that will be said honestly.

What if my pain is severe or new?

If your symptoms are new, severe, progressive, or meaningfully different from what you've known, a medical evaluation may be the right first step, and that will be said honestly during the call.
