Common questions

Before you book

What happens on the free consultation?

A one-on-one video call on Google Meet with a Doctor of Physical Therapy. We talk through your pain and what you've tried, explain what's likely going on, and if we're a good fit, map out your next step. The Meet link comes in your calendar invite.

Do you take insurance?

No, on purpose. Insurance-based care often means rushed sessions and minimal hands-on time. You can pay with your HSA/FSA card, and we can provide a superbill if your plan reimburses out-of-network care.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. No cost and no commitment. It's a chance to see if we're the right fit before you book any treatment.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on what we find. Many patients get results in a few sessions instead of 10-20+ visits, and we'll give you an honest plan once we've seen you.

I've already tried PT. Why would this be different?

Most of our patients tried PT, chiropractic or massage first. Here every visit is a full hour, one-on-one with a doctor of physical therapy, aimed at the cause of your pain rather than the symptoms.
