The MAT approach

A different approach to physical therapy

01
Find the weak link

When a muscle stops working well, other parts of your body tighten up and compensate. MAT tests muscles one by one to find which ones.

02
Turn it back on

Hands-on work helps your brain reconnect with those muscles, which reduces tightness, improves movement and decreases pain.

03
Built to last

The goal is long-term correction, not short-term relief: a stronger, more stable body that's more resilient to the next injury.

Before you book

Quick questions

What does it cost?+

The first assessment is $49 (normally $120 for a 60-minute session), paid when you book. After that, sessions are 60 minutes ($120), 90 minutes ($180) or 120 minutes ($240), depending on what you need.

What is MAT?+

Muscle Activation Techniques is a hands-on, non-medical method that finds muscles that aren't contracting well and helps your brain reconnect with them, so the rest of your body can stop compensating.

What can it help with?+

Back, neck, shoulder, hip and knee pain, sciatica, headaches, tennis elbow, plantar fasciitis, chronic ankle sprains, recovery from fractures or surgery, postpartum pain and weakness, and more.
