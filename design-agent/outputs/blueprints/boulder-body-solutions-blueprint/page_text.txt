Muscle Activation Technique (MAT) is a hands-on, non-medical method that helps muscles work better. When some muscles are weak, the body tightens up to protect itself. MAT finds and fixes these weak muscles by helping the brain connect with them again. This helps reduce tightness, improve movement, decrease pain and bring better balance and stability to the body.
