Approach: Most physical therapy focuses on the main symptom, pain. Aaron asks a different question: why isn't the body working the way it should? Using Muscle Activation Techniques he identifies the specific muscles that aren't activating properly, which force other parts of the body to compensate and eventually break down, then restores their function to decrease pain, improve movement and create lasting results. MAT is a hands-on, non-medical method: when some muscles are weak, the body tightens up to protect itself; MAT finds those muscles and helps the brain reconnect with them. It focuses on long-term correction, not short-term relief, and on making people more resilient to future injuries.

What MAT can help with: sciatica, back pain, neck pain, shoulder pain, rotator cuff injuries, headaches and chronic migraines, knee pain, hip pain, wrist and hand pain, tennis and golfer's elbow, plantar fasciitis, chronic ankle sprains, leg cramps, recovery from fractures or surgery, carpal tunnel, cervical radiculopathy, patellofemoral syndromes, posterior tibial tendonitis, metatarsalgia, neuromas, car accidents, fall prevention, postpartum pain and weakness. Also used before surgery (one patient used it in the 3 months before a total knee replacement) and for injury prevention and performance.
