00System overview

Every lead source runs into one agent. Nothing falls out of the system.

Your site invites people to book a free phone consultation, and every session is you, one-on-one, for 60 to 120 minutes. That's exactly where new patients slip through: you can't pick up the phone halfway through a MAT session. The engine keeps your personal approach and covers the gap. New ad leads, past patients, missed calls and Instagram DMs all land with one AI agent that replies in seconds, a voice agent calls new ad leads while they're still interested, and anyone who doesn't book, no-shows or finishes care gets recycled into reactivation instead of forgotten.

02Funnel

Why ads go to a funnel, not your website

boulderbodysolutions.com explains MAT well and it should stay: your story, how MAT works, the full list of what it helps with. That's perfect for someone researching. Someone who just clicked an ad needs one thing: a reason to book and an easy way to do it.

01

Only what matters

Just the information that gets someone to book: their problem, how MAT works, real reviews, what the visit covers and what it costs. Nothing else.

02

No distractions

No menu, no Learn page, no links away. Every extra page is a chance for them to wander off and forget about you.

03

One choice: book

Too many options causes decision paralysis. Every button on the page does the same thing: book the $49 assessment.

  • Six real patient reviews up front
  • Price and what MAT is answered early
  • One action: the $49 assessment
03AI appointment agent

An agent that answers while you're with a patient

You're one-on-one for an hour or more at a time, so calls and texts wait. This agent replies to every lead in seconds from your own website's facts, handles the questions people actually ask (what MAT is, what it costs, how it's different from regular PT) and books the $49 assessment. It's the same agent our clients run today. Try it from each place a lead can come from.

  • Knows MAT, your pricing, location and real reviews
  • Works through objections like price and "I've tried PT before", one at a time
  • Offers two open times and books the one they pick
  • Schedules a check-in when someone says "text me later"
  • Flags urgent or tricky conversations to you, and stops when someone isn't a fit or says no
  • Never gives medical advice or invents details

04AI voice agent

A voice agent that calls new leads while they're still interested

Meta leads go cold fast: someone fills out your form on a lunch break and has forgotten about it by dinner. Now, the moment a lead comes in, an AI voice agent calls them, has a natural conversation from the same facts as your text agent, and books the $49 assessment on your calendar. It works hand in hand with the SMS agent, so every lead gets a call and a text, and both land in one conversation you can read.

  • Calls every new Meta lead within about a minute of the form
  • Same knowledge and rules as the text agent: no medical advice, no made-up details
  • Books on your calendar, then the confirmation goes out by text
  • No answer? The SMS agent picks it up right away
  • Hands the lead to you whenever a person is needed
