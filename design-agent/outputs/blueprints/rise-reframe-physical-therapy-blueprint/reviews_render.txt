Lainey Lamb
8 months ago
 on 
Google
I came to BettyLou with chronic mid-back and chest pain that had been controlling my life since 2021. By the time I met her in July 2025, I had already gone through pretty much every “traditional” western medicine option available. I was actually scheduled to have a spinal cord stimulator implanted at the end of August, which meant I had one month to see if BettyLou could help. From the first appointment, it was obvious she was different. She was the first doctor who actually addressed and explained my nervous system. She checked my eyes, my pelvis, and started connecting dots no one else had ever talked about. She was incredibly attentive, very smart, and the information just flowed out of her with conviction. We made a plan, and I committed to working with her for four weeks. A big commitment, money-wise and time-wise. The nervous system focus was heavy, honestly heavier than I expected, but it started changing the way I understood my body and my pain. I legit had to treat the whole process like a second job. I’d go work my shift at Rezzo, then come home and spend two to three hours doing my homework from BettyLou and light training at the gym if I had the energy. Two weeks in, there was notable improvement, and it was motivating to keep investing the time. My pain went from a daily consistent 8/10 down to about a 4/10. The progress was slow but steady across the board. One of the biggest wins was finishing long work shifts, sometimes 12–16 hours on my feet, and still being able to stand up straight instead of ending the day in tears. I was also able to reduce one of my daily medication doses. That was HUGE. I felt stronger, mentally crawling out of a black hole, and the hope that was all but gone started coming back. Because of the progress we made, I ended up deciding not to move forward with the spinal cord stimulator and instead fully committed to a plethora of holistic and wellness modalities from August through December 2025. Now… I was in a lil car accident at the beginning of September that sent my nervous system right back into a severe fight-or-flight response, and my pain came back. But what hasn’t changed is BettyLou’s support. We moved from weekly visits to twice a week. Because R&R isn’t a traditional PT clinic, our one-on-one time meant Betty could be fully present and constantly adapting things to what my body is telling her. And so we trek on, continuing to work within whatever boundaries my body decides to set that day. On the harder days, the revitalization room in Temecula is a massive perk I take advantage of probably more than all her other patients combined. Beyond being knowledgeable and professional, she’s a good person. Def sassy, very honest, gracious to everyone, beautiful inside and out, and will literally pour herself out past the point of empty. Give, give, give, give, give. “Oh, did you get one??” *gives*. She’s constantly connecting people and valiantly building HER community FOR. YOU. So much so that she’s practically become a local celebrity. Our friendship has grown a lot through this process, and I’m blessed to know her. God so clearly aligned her path perfectly to be doing what she’s doing. If you have the pleasure of interacting with her, you best be addin that to your gratitude list. If you’re considering working with her, I strongly urge you, do it. You are worth the investment. You are in control of your time, finance, effort, and attitude. Make the move forward.
