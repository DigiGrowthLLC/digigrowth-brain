Rise & Reframe Physical Therapy (R&R PT) is a cash-based, one-on-one physical therapy and wellness practice in Temecula and Murrieta, California, owned and run by Dr. BettyLou DeYoung, PT, DPT, CPT, FRCms. Every session is 60 minutes of direct, one-on-one care with Dr. BettyLou: no aides, no double-booking, no rushing.

Dr. BettyLou: former competitive dancer. BS in Kinesiology (2018, Northern Arizona University), Doctorate of Physical Therapy (2021, University of St. Augustine), both with honors. Certified Dance Physical Therapist, NASM Certified Personal Trainer, Corrective Exercise Specialist, Blood Flow Restriction, LTAP Certified Practitioner, Functional Range Conditioning mobility specialist. Over seven years in health and wellness, inpatient and outpatient experience. Specializes in challenging cases: chronic pain, acute injuries, neurological conditions (MS, Parkinson's, spinal cord injury, traumatic brain injury, stroke), joint replacements, and athletes.

Approach: three steps. Find the root cause (comprehensive biomechanical assessment), reframe movement (whole-body plan tailored to the patient's goals, hands-on care), rise to performance (build strength and prevent re-injury). Looks at the whole picture: movement habits, stress, sleep, nervous system, history. Hands-on techniques include joint mobilization, myofascial release, soft-tissue massage, cupping, IASTM and e-stim. Home program and exercise videos through the BridgeAthletic app. The goal is for patients to need her less over time.

Who she helps: active adults, runners, athletes, lifters (never told to just stop lifting), active women, new moms (postpartum), busy professionals, active parents, dancers and cheerleaders, kids and teens ages 6 to 18.

What she treats: back and neck pain, knee and hip pain, shoulder, elbow, wrist, ankle, chronic pain, acute injury, post-surgical recovery, headaches, hypermobility, sports injuries, pelvic health, neurological rehabilitation, reconditioning and balance, injury prevention, return to sport.

First visit offer: the $49 New Patient Assessment (normally $150), one-on-one with Dr. BettyLou, paid when booking. Only 10 open each month. At the first visit she listens to the patient's full story, does a physical assessment, and they leave knowing what's driving their symptoms and with a plan. Prices for ongoing care are not listed on the website; the team walks patients through exactly what to expect before they commit. Free discovery calls are also available for anyone with questions before booking.

Insurance: cash-based, does not accept insurance, on purpose: insurance decides how long patients are seen and how many visits they get. No doctor's referral needed.

Revitalization Room (Temecula, Tuesdays and Thursdays 8am to 6pm): red light therapy and PEMF therapy, $129 a month unlimited or $20 a session.
