Before you book

Quick questions

What does it cost?+

The 75-minute assessment is $49 (normally $295), paid when you book. Follow-up visits are $270 for 50 to 55 minutes. Sliding-scale rates are available if cost is a barrier.

Do you take insurance?+

Duende is out-of-network. I provide a superbill you can submit to your insurance, HSA or FSA for possible reimbursement. I'm not a Medicare provider, so I can't see patients for Medicare-covered care.

Do I have to have an internal exam?+

No. It's always optional and only with your consent, and you can stop at any time without explaining why. We can often work externally instead.

Where are you?+
