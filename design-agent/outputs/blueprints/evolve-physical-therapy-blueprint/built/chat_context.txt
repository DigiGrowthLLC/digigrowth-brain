Evolve Physical Therapy, LLC is a cash-pay, one-on-one physical therapy practice in Richland, Washington (Tri-Cities), owned and run by Dr. Morgan Gese, PT, DPT, OCS. Tagline: "From Pain, to Performance." Fitness-forward physical therapy for active adults who want to move past pain and get back to lifting, training and the activities they love. Every visit is a full 60 minutes, one-on-one with Dr. Morgan, a Doctor of Physical Therapy, every visit. No aides, no double-booking.

Dr. Morgan: Tri-Cities native. Doctorate in Physical Therapy from the University of St. Augustine for Health Sciences, Bachelor's in Exercise Science from Eastern Washington University. Board-certified Orthopedic Clinical Specialist (OCS). Endorsed in Dry Needling and Spinal Manipulation in Washington. More than a decade treating orthopedic injuries, pre-op and post-op care, and helping people avoid surgery when possible. Former multi-sport community college and Division 1 basketball player. Works out at Orangetheory and CrossFit, plays softball, basketball and pickleball. Loves working with current and former athletes and active people of all levels.

Approach, three steps: 1) Find the pain generator: thorough interview, assessment and movement analysis. 2) Ease the irritation: specific manual therapy. 3) Evolve your potential: build strength, power and endurance so the pain doesn't return and re-injury is far less likely. "Wait and see" isn't a plan, and rest alone rarely fixes what's holding training back. The plan is built around how the patient actually trains.

Services: manual therapy, joint mobilization, spinal manipulation and mobilization, dry needling, myofascial decompression (cupping), soft tissue work, exercise prescription, video analysis (gait analysis, lifting technique, performance coaching). Personalized home exercise program with video recordings. Dry Needling Recovery Sessions (30 min, $85) are available only after an initial evaluation.

Conditions: back pain, hip pain and impingement, knee pain (including meniscus injuries and people not back to pre-injury or post-surgery function), foot, heel and ankle pain (plantar fasciitis, Achilles), shoulder and elbow pain (lifting overhead, gripping, swinging a club or paddle), neck pain and headaches that come from the neck. Also runners, lifters, CrossFit and pickleball players, pregnancy and postpartum return to exercise.

First visit offer: the $49 New Patient Assessment (normally $175), a full hour one-on-one with Dr. Morgan, paid when booking. Only 10 open each month. It includes a comprehensive evaluation and movement assessment with video analysis to find the source of pain, treatment, and a clear plan. Regular visits are $175 per visit, with lower-cost package options if bought up front. A free 15-minute phone consultation is also available for anyone with questions before booking.

Insurance: does not bill insurance, on purpose, so care follows the patient's needs, not insurance limits. Superbills provided for out-of-network reimbursement (most non-Medicare patients in WA can submit self-claims). HSA and FSA accepted. No referral needed: physical therapy is direct access in Washington.

Cancellation policy: no-show or cancellation within 12 hours of the appointment is a $25 fee. What to wear: workout clothes; shorts for lower-body issues; for shoulder or neck issues, a top that shows the shoulder blades.
