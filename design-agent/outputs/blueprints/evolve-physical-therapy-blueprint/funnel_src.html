Before you book

Quick questions

What does it cost?+

Our first visit is normally $175. The New Patient Assessment is $49, paid when you book. We only open 10 each month so every new patient gets a full hour with Dr. Morgan. If you continue, she walks you through visit and package pricing before you commit. No surprise bills.

Do you take insurance?+

We don't bill insurance, on purpose: it lets your plan follow what your body needs, not what's covered. We're happy to give you a superbill to submit for out-of-network reimbursement, and HSA/FSA cards are welcome.

Do I need a referral?+

No. Physical therapy is direct access in Washington, so you can book and get started right away.
