Exceptional Solutions PT (Exceptional Solutions Physical Therapy), Grand Rapids, Michigan. A new, owner-run clinic specializing in physical therapy for chronic pain, hypermobility and Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome (EDS/hEDS).

Clinician: Samantha Newell, DPT, the owner and currently the only employee (no PTAs, never PT techs). She has Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome herself (her site says EDS and does not name the type, so never say hEDS about her) and a lifetime of personal experience with hypermobility and chronic pain. Completed the EDS ECHO training for practitioners through the Ehlers-Danlos Society. Calvin College graduate (kinesiology, psychology, French), DPT graduated 2022.

Approach: one-on-one, gentle, individualized care with no standard protocols (she does not use the Muldowney protocol). Movement re-training and muscle control first, before strengthening, to avoid pain and injury in hypermobile bodies. Combines traditional physical therapy with somatic and nervous system re-education and holistic methods. Trauma-informed and neurodivergent-friendly. Listens to the patient's story and believes them. Great fit for people whose past physical therapy didn't help or made things worse.

Conditions: chronic pain (pain lasting 3+ months), hypermobility, Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome, joint pain and instability, pre- and post-op care, chronic back pain. Also sees kids and teens with joint pain. Takes out-of-state patients (the clinic is about 10 minutes from motels and restaurants).
