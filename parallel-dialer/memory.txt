# Parallel Dialer Memory

Target niche: Independent mobile and in-home veterinary practices
Active market: All US states — priority on Sun Belt (FL, TX, GA, SC, NC, AZ, TN, VA)
Primary metric: Decision makers reached (practice owner / veterinarian-owner)

## Rules

- Grade A leads are dialed first, then B, C, D
- Max 3 call attempts per lead before SMS handoff
- Machine detection is enabled — voicemails are not counted as a DM reached
- Do not dial on Saturdays or Sundays
- Discount Tires shift days: reduce session length or skip if Dylan is working

## Ideal Prospect

- Dr. [Name] — independent DVM who owns a mobile or in-home vet practice
- Companion animals (dogs and cats) as primary focus
- 1–6 staff, suburban area, no in-house marketing
- Growing through referrals only — no paid ads, no funnel

## Pitch Frame

- Offer: Done-for-you Meta ads system running a complimentary in-home wellness visit offer
- Delivers 20–40 new client appointments in 6 weeks
- She confirms appointments — nothing else required
- ROI: 20 appts × $200 avg = $4,000+ in new revenue, many become recurring ($500–$1k+/yr LTV)

## Common Objections

- "I get enough from referrals" → Average practice loses ~100 active clients/year to churn. Referrals replace some — this adds a predictable layer on top.
- "I don't have time for an agency" → Done-for-you. She doesn't manage ads, funnel, or follow-up. She just shows up for appointments.
- "I've never done paid ads" → Same strategy vets have used for decades with new client promotions, just systematized on Meta.
- "How much does it cost?" → Hold until ROI is established. Ask: "If we put 20 new client appointments on your calendar this month, what does that mean in revenue for you?"

## Qualifying Questions

1. "Are you currently running any paid ads or is growth mostly coming from referrals right now?"
2. "How's your schedule looking most weeks — pretty full, or are there gaps you'd like to fill?"
3. "If I could put 20 new client appointments on your calendar in the next 6 weeks, is that something you'd want to explore?"

## Disqualifiers (End Call Politely)

- Brick-and-mortar only clinic (no mobile component)
- Corporate-owned or franchise location
- Solo operator with no support staff
- Already has in-house marketing or a marketing coordinator
- Explicitly at capacity and not looking to grow
